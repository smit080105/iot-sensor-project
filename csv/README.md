# How to build devices.csv now

The CSV is the registry. **You** choose `serial_number`, `product_type`,
and `dongle_id` — nothing is auto-generated by the backend anymore.

## Steps

1. Make sure `iot-sensor-project` is running (`docker compose up --build`
   in `docker/`).
2. Add a row to `devices.csv` with the MAC address of the device and
   whatever `serial_number` / `dongle_id` you want to assign it:
   ```
   mac_address,serial_number,product_type,dongle_id
   74:4D:BD:AA:C0:F0,SN0001,AHT10,DGL0001
   ```
   Both `mac_address` and `dongle_id` are required — a row missing either
   is rejected.
3. Upload that CSV through the dashboard (**UPLOAD DEVICES CSV** panel).
   You should see `Added: 1 · Now registered: 1`.
4. Tell her (or have her already know) the `mac_address` and shared
   token to publish on `DEVICE_reg`. When she does, the backend looks
   that MAC up in this CSV-backed registry and publishes the matching
   row — `serial_number`, `dongle_id`, `product_type` — back on
   `DEVICE_regok`. Watch the **DEVICE_reg ATTEMPTS** panel on the
   dashboard to see it happen live, including the raw mac/token
   received and the result.
5. Once she starts publishing telemetry to `device/DGL0001/telemetry`
   (using the `dongle_id` from your CSV row), readings show up on the
   dashboard under **LIVE SENSORS** and **RAW FEED**.

If a MAC isn't in the CSV yet when she publishes `DEVICE_reg`, the
attempt is logged as "not in CSV registry" and no `DEVICE_regok` is
sent — upload the CSV first, then have her retry.
